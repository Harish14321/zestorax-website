About ZestoraX

Building smart tools to help e-commerce businesses thrive

Our Story

ZestoraX was founded with a simple mission: to help Shopify merchants understand their true profitability and make smarter business decisions.

We noticed that many store owners were flying blind - tracking revenue but not true profit. They didn't know which products were actually making money, where they were losing margins, or how to optimize their operations.

That's why we built ProfitGuard Pro - a powerful analytics tool that goes beyond revenue to show you your real net profit after all costs. And we're just getting started.

Based in Bangalore, India, we're a small but passionate team dedicated to building tools that make a real difference for e-commerce businesses worldwide.
