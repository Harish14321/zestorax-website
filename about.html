About ZestoraX

Building smart tools to help e-commerce businesses thrive

Our Story

ZestoraX was born from a simple observation: most Shopify merchants know their revenue, but have no idea what they actually keep as profit.

We saw store owners celebrating big sales days, only to wonder at the end of the month where all the money went. Fees, refunds, discounts, and COGS were silently eating into their margins — and they had no way to track it.

That's why we built ProfitGuard Pro. Not another analytics dashboard showing vanity metrics, but a true profit calculator that shows you exactly what you made on every order, every product, every day.

Today, we serve 500+ merchants who finally know their real numbers. And we're just getting started.

Our Mission

Help every Shopify merchant understand their true profitability

We believe that data should empower, not overwhelm. Every tool we build is designed to answer one simple question: "Am I actually making money?"

Revenue is just the beginning of the story. True profit — what you keep after all costs — is what matters. We're on a mission to make that number visible, actionable, and easy to understand for every merchant.

We don't just track numbers. We help you understand them, act on them, and grow your business profitably.
