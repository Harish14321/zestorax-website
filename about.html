About ZestoraX

Focused Shopify apps, each solving one real merchant problem — nothing more

Our Story

ZestoraX started with a simple observation: most Shopify merchants know their revenue, but have no idea what they actually keep as profit — and the tools built to help usually bury that answer under features they don't need, at prices gated behind tiers they don't want.

Our first app, ProfitGuard Pro, was built to answer one question honestly: what did you actually make, after every cost? Not another dashboard full of vanity metrics — a real profit calculator, nothing more.

That same idea — one app, one real problem, solved properly — is now the whole company. ShelfClear, RestockClear, RewardClear, and ShipClear each exist because one specific merchant problem (dead stock, stockouts, loyalty, shipping delays) deserved its own focused answer, not another feature bolted onto something else.

Today we have one app live on the Shopify App Store and four more in final testing. We're just getting started.

Harish Babu Sammeta

Founder

Harish holds an M.Tech in Automotive Engineering and a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering. He started his career as an embedded software engineer and currently works as an automotive engineer — building ZestoraX's apps alongside that, plus freelance application development and electronics projects, in his own time.

ZestoraX started in 2026 as a way to bring that same hands-on engineering discipline to Shopify tooling: solve one real problem well, ship it honestly, and skip the rest.

Our Mission

Build focused Shopify apps that solve one real problem, honestly

We believe tools should empower, not overwhelm. Every app we build is designed around one specific merchant question — "Am I actually making money?", "What's about to run out?", "Is this customer coming back?" — and answers it well, instead of trying to answer everything.

That means flat pricing with nothing gated behind a higher tier, and numbers we can actually back — a plain estimate instead of a confident-looking guess we can't stand behind.

We don't just track numbers. We help you understand them, act on them, and grow your business with confidence.
